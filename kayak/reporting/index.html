Kayak Reporting

Kayak Reporting records game activity in the game owner's Google spreadsheet and emails the owner when activity is recorded. It helps the owner check that the game is being used and that reporting is working.

Players do not sign into Google or authorize this connection. Google authorization is for the owner's reporting account only.

What is recorded

Reports include game starts, level starts and level completions, together with the score, level, game version, timestamp, IP address and basic browser/device details.

Google access

The connection requests Google Sheets access to append rows to the existing Kayak usage spreadsheet, and Gmail send access to send notifications to the owner's fixed email address. It does not request Gmail inbox-reading permission.

Google's Sheets permission covers all spreadsheets in the authorized account. The reporting service uses one configured spreadsheet and does not offer players a way to choose a destination or recipient.

The Cloudflare service limits notification attempts to at most 100 per UTC day. Spreadsheet logging continues if that email limit is reached.

Kayak v1.1.38 and later report through Cloudflare Workers. Older copies continue to use Pipedream while players update; the old workflow remains active during that transition.
